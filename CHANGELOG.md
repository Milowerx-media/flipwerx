# Changelog

The version shows in the top bar of the app. Click it to see this list inside Loopwerx.

## 0.9.0 (2026-11-25)

First numbered version. It includes everything built so far, plus:

- A version number in the top bar, a What's new list, and a notice when a new version loads.
- One click selects just the fill or just the stroke of a shape, and a double-click selects both. Delete removes only the part you picked.
- 1 and 2 zoom the timeline after you click in it.
- Frame Picker window (F): see every frame inside a symbol and click one to show it.
- HTML panels inside `.zxp` extensions open in their own window.
- Saving and exporting work in an ordinary browser tab as well as in Claude.
