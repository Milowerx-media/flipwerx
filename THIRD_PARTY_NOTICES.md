# Third-party software

Loopwerx loads these libraries from a CDN at the moment a feature needs them. They aren't copied into this repository.

| Library | Version | Used for | Licence | Loaded from |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [gifenc](https://github.com/mattdesl/gifenc) | 1.0.3 | GIF export | MIT | jsDelivr |
| [mp4-muxer](https://github.com/Vanilagy/mp4-muxer) | 5.2.1 | MP4 export | MIT | jsDelivr |
| [JSZip](https://github.com/Stuk/jszip) | 3.10.1 | Reading `.zxp` and `.mxp` extension packages | MIT or GPL-3.0-or-later (used under MIT) | cdnjs |
| [ag-psd](https://github.com/Agamnentzar/ag-psd) | 30.0.0 | Reading Photoshop `.psd` files | MIT | jsDelivr |

The interface uses the [IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono](https://github.com/IBM/plex) fonts from Google Fonts, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Each library's full licence text is in its own repository and npm package.
